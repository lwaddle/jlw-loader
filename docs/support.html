Common Questions

How do I get an access code?
Access codes are provided by your organization's administrator. Contact them directly if you need a new code.

What USB drives are supported?
The app works with FAT32-formatted USB drives connected via a Lightning or USB-C adapter.

Why does the app need to erase my USB drive?
The avionics system requires a specific file structure on the drive. The app wipes and writes the exact layout needed for a successful database load.
